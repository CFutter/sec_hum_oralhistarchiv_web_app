About the Archive

The Digital Oral History Archive provides a unified search interface for interview metadata from oral history research collections at the University of Zurich.

What is this?

This web application allows researchers to search, filter, and explore metadata from recorded oral history interviews. It aggregates data from multiple source systems into a single, consistent interface.

The archive does not store or serve interview recordings directly. Depending on access level and source, recordings are available through the originating repository.

Access Levels

public
Metadata visible to all visitors. No login required.
registered
Extended metadata visible to registered academic users.
restricted
Full metadata available only to vetted researchers with approved access.
