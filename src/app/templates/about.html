What is this?

This web application allows researchers to search, filter, and explore metadata from recorded oral history interviews. It aggregates data from multiple source systems into a single, consistent interface.

The archive does not store or serve interview recordings directly. Depending on access level and source, recordings are available through the originating repository.

Metadata access tiers

Everyone can see titles and basic catalogue details for datasets listed in the catalogue. Datasets hidden by an administrator are excluded at every tier.

Other metadata is shown field by field. Where a dataset, a field and its source have different access requirements, the strictest applies. A public-tier dataset can therefore still contain restricted fields.

public
Metadata fields requiring public access are available without signing in.
registered
Users with registered access can view fields requiring public or registered access.
vetted
Users with vetted access can view fields requiring public, registered or vetted access in listed datasets.

Registered and vetted access must be granted by an administrator. Access to recordings is governed by the originating repository.
